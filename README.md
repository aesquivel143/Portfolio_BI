### 3. Supply Chain & Inventory Management Dashboard
- **Problem:** High variability in lead times, bottlenecks in the supply flow, and accumulation of obsolete/non-moving inventory. Applied Yellow Belt concepts to map the value stream, identify waste from waiting and overstock, and define Key Performance Indicators (COF Rate, stock age).
- **Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Advanced Excel, Lean Supply Chain & Six Sigma Principles.
- **Results:** Unified and standardized monitoring of warehouse efficiency (86.17%) and average lead time (3.5 days). Serves as a Control-phase dashboard to detect deviations in fulfillment rate (COF Rate 94.56%), prevent inventory obsolescence, and accelerate operational throughput.
