## Featured Projects

### 1. OEE & Reliability Dashboard - Bagging Train Line (Indelpro)
- **Problem:** Lack of centralized visibility into Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE), equipment operational status, and downtime tracking across the production line.
- **Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, SQL, SharePoint Lists.
- **Results:** Real-time consolidation of OEE, Availability, Performance, and Quality metrics. Integrated live machine status monitoring and a fault traceability module (open, in-progress, and resolved) categorized by equipment and fault code.

### 2. Commercial, Sales & Marketing Performance Dashboard
- **Problem:** Fragmented sales, marketing, and customer data, making it difficult to evaluate Return on Investment (ROI) and maintain a unified view of the sales pipeline.
- **Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Advanced Excel, SQL.
- **Results:** Real-time centralization of MRR, conversion rate, and pipeline efficiency, enabling optimization of acquisition metrics (CAC, CPL, ROI) and regional performance analysis.

### 3. Supply Chain & Inventory Management Dashboard
- **Problem:** Lack of visibility into delivery lead times, supply failures, inventory aging, and overall warehouse stock control.
- **Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Advanced Excel.
- **Results:** Unified tracking of Customer Order Fulfillment (COF Rate: 94.56%), warehouse efficiency (86.17%), and average delivery time (3.5 days), enabling the identification of supply bottlenecks and in-depth analysis of inventory distribution by age range.
